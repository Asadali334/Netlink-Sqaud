Step-by-Step Network Creation

Follow these expanded steps to design and deploy a network.

1. Decide scope & devices

How many clients, servers, Wi-Fi clients, printers, etc.

2. Choose cabling

Cat6 for LAN, fiber for long uplinks and backbone.

3. IP planning

Use subnets, DHCP pools, static IPs for infra devices.

4. Security & monitoring

VLANs, ACLs, logging, SNMP monitoring and regular audits.
